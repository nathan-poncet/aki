For each merge request, pull request and ticket you are involved in, aki looks at who spoke last in every thread, the state of reviews and approvals, the pipeline, the branch, and the ticket's status. Each fact that asks something of someone becomes a reason. A task is listed once, under its most pressing reason; the others show in the detail pane.

One reason to do is enough to put the whole task in To do.

Change a rule

Any rule takes three settings. A lower priority comes first.

[rules.draft_to_finish]
enabled = false

[rules.checks_running]
bucket = "todo"
priority = 45

aki rules prints every rule as it applies. An unknown rule name is an error, so a typo never silently disables a rule.

Threads

Teams differ on who closes a review thread once it is answered.

[threads]
who_resolves = "reviewer"
ignore_bots = true
ValueEffect
"reviewer"Whoever opened the thread checks the answer and resolves it. An answered thread you opened is Resolve threads; one you answered is Awaiting reply.
"author"The author resolves threads once addressed.
"anyone"No convention: only who spoke last counts.

With ignore_bots, a bot's comment never gives anyone the next step.

Re-reviews

After your first review, any of these triggers makes a re-review due:

[re_review]
triggers = ["author_replied", "new_commits", "re_requested", "approval_reset"]
after_approval = false
TriggerFires when
author_repliedSomeone answered in a thread you take part in.
new_commitsCommits were pushed after your last review activity.
re_requestedYour review was requested again.
approval_resetYour approval was reset or dismissed.
all_threads_answeredEvery thread you opened has been answered or resolved. Not in the default list.

after_approval = true lets a re-review be due on a change you already approved.

Drafts

[drafts]
mine = "todo"
others = "wait"
SettingValueEffect
mine"todo"Your drafts are listed with everything else that waits on you.
"apart"They stay on the board but leave To do, the counts and the notifications. They show only in panes filtered on is:draft.
"ignore"They are left off the board until they are ready.
others"wait"A draft you review is listed as waiting on its author.
"review"It is treated like any other review.
"ignore"It is left off the board.

To give your drafts a pane of their own, set mine = "apart" and add a pane filtered on is:draft role:author. See Spaces.

Nudge

[nudge]
after_days = 7

A waiting item that nobody touched for that many days becomes Nudge in To do. 0, the default, turns it off.

Ticket statuses

[tickets]
waiting_statuses = ["blocked", "on hold", "in review", "waiting"]

A ticket in one of these statuses (compared without case) is Blocked, in Waiting. So is a ticket with an "is blocked by" link.

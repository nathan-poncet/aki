Move

KeyDoes
↑ ↓, j kThe task, within the pane.
PgUp PgDn, Home EndTen tasks; the first, the last.
← →, h l, TabThe pane.
1-9, , .The space.

The mouse works too: click a space, a pane or a task; scroll a pane.

A task

KeyDoes
Enter, oOpen it in the browser, on the thread itself when there is one.
iExplain it: why it is listed, how its urgency adds up, what every mark on a line means.
aOpen your coding agent on it, briefed, in its clone.
pSet it aside: t tomorrow, w a week, 1-9 days, m until it moves. On a task already aside, bring it back.

The pane

KeyDoes
/Filter it, applied as you type.
bIts side: to do → waiting → both.
gIts grouping: none → repo → kind → source → person → role.
s vIts order: urgency → rule → waiting → updated → created; reverse it.
n xNew pane beside this one; close it.
Shift+← →, H LMove it among the others.
m then 1-9Send it to another space.
eName it.
zZoom it; Esc leaves.
dShow or hide the detail pane.

The space

KeyDoes
IExplain it: how it is made, its panes, the repositories it holds.
tIts layout: columns → rows → master → grid.
N R XNew space; rename; remove (asks first).
SIts scope: what belongs to it at all.
< >Move it among the others.

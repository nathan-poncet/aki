aki list

aki list --todo --space Work --filter "repo:web -is:draft" --sort waiting
OptionMeaning
--todo, --waitingOnly one side.
--space NAMEOnly what belongs to one of your spaces.
--filter QUERYA filter.
--sort ORDERurgency (default), rule, waiting, updated, created.
--reverseThe opposite order.
--freshAsk the sources even if a recent reading is cached.
--jsonMachine-readable.
--countOne line.

In a prompt or a status bar

aki list --count
11 to do · 13 waiting · 3 set aside · 19 drafts apart

A reading younger than cache_seconds (60 by default) is reused, so the command returns at once when called often.

Exit status

StatusMeaning
0Done. aki list succeeds as long as something could be shown, even from the cache.
1aki list: no source could be read and nothing was remembered. aki doctor: a source failed.
2A command or the configuration could not be understood.
